Roadmap to Platinum about 200 hours · difficulty 4/10 · PowerPyx

  1. 1 · Story first (60–80 h)Play the chapters in order and finish every named mission. Do the Chapter 1 home meditation the first time it is offered — it is the one possibly missable trophy. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go; progress counts later.
  2. 2 · 100% checklist (~100 h)After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Thresholds are lenient (about 60% of true completion). You need 70 of 80 Challenges, so skip the hardest ones.
  3. 3 · Strangers & side strands (~10 h)Finish 8 stranger strands and the character quests (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret). Most trophies pop automatically by now.
  4. 4 · CleanupGrind the counters left over: 250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles, card sets, robberies and money totals. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off.
  5. 5 · Platinum“Vice Got a Price!” pops the moment the other 54 are earned. There is no mission replay, so play story missions carefully the first time.

⚠ The one missable to watch: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot; the first meditation is offered at home in Chapter 1 and may become unavailable after that chapter. Source: PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide. Every other trophy is confirmed unmissable or not flagged by any published guide.
