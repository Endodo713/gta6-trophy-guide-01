# SOURCES — gta6-trophy-guide-01

Full links for the sources named inside the app. The app itself (everything
under `app/`) contains **no URLs** — only guide names — so it stays fully
offline. This file lives at the repo root, outside `app/`.

## Trophy list (names, descriptions, grades)

- Exophase — GTA 6 trophies (PSN mirror, primary tracker for descriptions):
  https://www.exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/
- PC Gamer — GTA 6 trophies list (names/grades corroboration; article title
  "GTA 6 trophies list" on pcgamer.com — no direct link recorded, named only).
- PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (names/grades, roadmap figures,
  missable flag, difficulty/time estimates):
  https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-grand-theft-auto-vi-trophy-guide-roadmap/
- GTABase — GTA 6 Achievements & Trophies list (names/grades, per-trophy tips):
  https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-achievements-trophies-list-ps5-xbox-series

## What each source was used for

- **Exophase**: all 55 descriptions verbatim, in order; the count (55) and the
  grade split (1 platinum / 2 gold / 6 silver / 46 bronze).
- **PowerPyx**: roadmap (4/10 difficulty, ~200 hours, 1 playthrough, no mission
  replay, cheats disable trophies, 100% checklist location and lenient
  thresholds, 70 of 80 Challenges), the single possibly-missable trophy
  (Positive Mental Attitude), and the 5 trophies whose unlock method it lists
  as not yet known (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess,
  The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin).
- **GTABase / PC Gamer**: independent corroboration of names and grades; GTABase
  also for per-trophy guidance wording (rewritten in our own words in the app).
- **Unverified, not used**: Gamerscore per trophy (sources conflict), hidden
  trophy counts (28 vs 15 conflict), the uncensored name of trophy #4
  (printed censored everywhere checkable — kept as “T**s Ass Drug Money”).

## Verification notes (19 Nov 2026)

- No PlayStation-official or Rockstar trophy list exists as of 19 Nov 2026
  (both checked, neither publishes one). Names/grades are therefore
  Tier-2-corroborated, not direct-primary-confirmed; the app's footer says so.
- The app's guides are original wording. Nothing is copied from the sources
  above; each guide names the source its facts come from.
